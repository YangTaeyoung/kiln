# Kiln 0.1.21 verification

Includes [saved-profile sign-in recovery](0.1.19-status.md) and
[automatic usage updates](account-usage.md). The private 0.1.20 candidate was
superseded before publication; its immutable tag and archive remain unchanged.

## Source verification

- `kiln-accounts --lib`: **78 passed** with isolated credentials, fake CLI
  processes and loopback HTTP. Includes active/inactive accounts, background
  cycles without settings, worker shutdown, retry delays, new-token retry,
  repeated failures retaining last-good data, deletion during a slow response,
  persisted check timestamps and cross-account credential attribution guards.
- A controlled clock reproduces an earlier account's 15-second then 1-second
  request latency. The later account becomes due at 315 seconds, rather than
  waiting for the 600-second batch. Staged logins, provider backoff and missing
  credentials do not cause per-second retry loops.
- Real loopback HTTP covers read-only GET/no body, no redirect forwarding,
  Retry-After, private-error redaction and bounded oversized chunked responses.
- Actual GPU account cards fit 420px at 130% scale in all four languages.
- `kiln-editor --test ui_quick_open`: **5 passed**. Reopening now waits for
  the unfiltered asynchronous results instead of assuming four frames suffice;
  the selected path and Escape-close assertions remain intact.
- Independent hostile review found and repaired account attribution, narrow
  English card overlap and the later-account scheduling issue. Final source
  re-review found no remaining P1/P2 issue in this scope.
- No real account tokens, Keychain entries or provider APIs were used in tests.
  The owner's installed app, sessions and live daemon remain unchanged.
