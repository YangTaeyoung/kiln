# Kiln 0.2.4

- Account settings show credit figures: Claude extra usage for the billing period against its
  monthly limit (or off), and Codex credits and available reset credits with the nearest expiry.
- Redeem a Codex reset credit from the account menu after confirming it, or from the limit
  notification when no other account has room left. Kiln never redeems a credit on its own.
- Codex usage history: lifetime and peak daily tokens, streaks and the last 14 days.
- Reorder workspaces by dragging them in the sidebar, or with Move Up and Move Down in the
  workspace menu.

Windows archives remain unsigned by Authenticode; in-app updates use the existing Ed25519
update signature.
