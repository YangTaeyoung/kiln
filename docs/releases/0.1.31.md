# Kiln 0.1.31

- Show the result of a click immediately. Selecting a tab, closing a panel or
  splitting one is drawn in the same moment instead of waiting for the next
  timer tick, which could take up to a second while the app was otherwise idle.
- Cut the conversation-sync worker's background CPU use. Linked conversation
  groups and their canonical destinations are computed once per scan instead
  of once per conversation; on a state of about 1,200 linked conversations the
  work after each scan went from about 1.2 seconds to about 20 milliseconds.
- Stop showing conversations that were continued separately in both CLIs as
  failures. Both histories are kept, so they no longer raise the workspace
  warning badge or count as needing attention; the sync dialog explains that
  each one opens from its own CLI's /resume.
- Say where to continue a synchronized conversation (which CLI and account),
  and move the conversation ID copy buttons, now labeled with their CLI and
  account, into **Conversation details**.
