# Kiln 0.1.19

## Account sign-in recovery

- Sign in again from a saved Claude Code or Codex account without deleting its profile.
- Keep the profile name, ID, order and conversation history. Sign-in to a different account is rejected.
- Show a direct recovery action when local credentials are missing or incomplete.
- Stage the new login until you choose **Apply**; cancellation and failed attempts preserve existing credentials and running panels.
- Keep recovery controls accessible in narrow settings windows, with Korean, English, Japanese and Simplified Chinese translations.

See [agent accounts](../accounts.md) for the sign-in and recovery flow. The official CLI owns token refresh; local credential status does not establish online account validity.
