# Kiln 0.1.30

- Stop re-reading failed linked conversations in the background. A conversation
  whose linked histories diverged is checked again when it or a linked history
  changes, or after an hour, instead of every two minutes. Superseded snapshots
  follow the same rule.
- Keep conversation-sync results across restarts and updates: the sync worker
  saves them when Kiln closes, and changing linked workspaces no longer discards
  them, so Kiln does not read every linked history again after launching.
